These are the six photos of the Laie Temple, and each image is under one folder, or div. The sidebar and page of images are in another folder or div.

Three things that looked wrong or hard to use:

First, the links were to small to press on, so we have to make the links bigger so we don't fat thumb the links. Second, the images were organized terribly when using a mobile device to view images. They looked like they were either too big, or gave us a hard time to navigate. Finally, the images were too chunky, so we had to shrink their size a little bit. Additionally, we needed a breakpoint so the images do not collide with each other when using a mobile device. The breaking point is 600px.
