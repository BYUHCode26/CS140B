3. What are special entity characters?

Special Entities are functions or commands that allow you to add special characters as text on the webpage. For example: "_, , &, etc."

4. What are phrase tags?

Phrase Tags are basically tags you can use as functions for your text. They do not affect the position, or the size of your text, just the text appearence, or font.

5. What is the difference between OL and UL Tags?

UL Tags acts like a text breaker, except they include a bullet point after the text when included with the LI Tag. OL Tags also act like line breakers, but include numbered bullets when associated with LI Tags.
