Interests Hero

Why do you like it?

My interest is in piano, because it is a very complex musical instrument that deals with chords, and sounds really good in general. This 'percussion' instrument is played mainly in classical, jazz, and many popular songs. It allows a lot of creativity, and is really fun to play. What makes it different from other instruments is its versatility. It has many octaves, can accompany many instruments, sets the mood for many different song genres, and overall is very fun to play.

Back on Maui, I would play in my school jazz club, and perform for many gigs. We would play all over Maui with famous musicians like Eric Miyashiro, and other jazz pianists. Although these gigs are usually small, we practice every week with new music. We are always practicing or improving on something new.
