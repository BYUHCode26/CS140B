Hero

Eric Miyashiro

Eric Miyashiro is one of the greatest trumpet players, especially because he contributed to many great pieces like Bo-bomb Battlefield. Although he is musically talented in trumpet, he has knowledge in every piece of instrument like the piano. What made him my hero is when he performed with us during the pep band concerts, and jazz gigs. With his trumpet, he showed us how professional, and how much experiene he had by playing louder than three student trumpet players combined.
